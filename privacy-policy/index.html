Privacy Policy

The short version: our tools run in your browser. Your photos are processed on your device and are never uploaded to us or anyone else. We don't have accounts, so we don't have your personal data.

Photos you upload into the tools

When you use the crochet pattern generator or cross stitch pattern maker, your image is loaded into your browser's memory and converted to a chart locally, on your device. The image is not transmitted to any server, not stored, and is gone when you close the tab.

Analytics and cookies

We may use privacy-respecting, aggregate web analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) that do not use cookies and do not identify individuals. We do not run advertising cookies at this time; if this changes, this policy will be updated first.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. When you visit, Cloudflare processes your request (including your IP address) to serve the page, as any web host does. We do not share data with any other third party.
