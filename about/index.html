About Looprel

Looprel makes free chart tools for people who make things with yarn and thread. We started with one simple idea: turning a photo into a crochet or cross stitch chart shouldn't require an account, a subscription, or uploading your family photos to someone's server.

Everything on this site runs entirely in your browser. Your photos are processed on your own device and never leave it. There is no login, and the charts you generate are yours — print them, stitch them, gift them.

What we make

  • Crochet pattern generator — photo to C2C, tapestry or filet chart
  • Cross stitch pattern maker — photo to symbol chart
  • Chart reading guide — how the three chart styles work
